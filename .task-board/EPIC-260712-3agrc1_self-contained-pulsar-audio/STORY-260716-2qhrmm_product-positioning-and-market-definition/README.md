# Product positioning and market definition

## Description
Define Pulsar Audio as a private shared-presence service between homes, with a precise audience, competitive frame, capability status and evidence-bounded roadmap.

## Scope
Product narrative, primary and adjacent segments, alternatives and competitors, present and committed capabilities, future hypotheses, provider integration feasibility, success signals and product guardrails. No application implementation.

## Acceptance Criteria
A reviewable Russian-language product document distinguishes current reality from committed and future scope; names the target users and non-targets; compares the closest alternatives using primary sources; evaluates official and maintained open-source integration routes for Spotify, Apple Music and Yandex Music; records legal, ToS, licensing and reliability uncertainty; and links to the engineering source of truth.
