# Freeze Air lifecycle, activation, alias and policy contracts

## Description
Resolve every multi-Air, join-in-progress, leave and rollback semantic before schema or runtime work can choose incompatible behavior.

## Scope
Define exact app and Telegram operations and payloads for create, list saved Airs, invite, consume, joining-primary confirm, decline or withdraw, activate or switch, leave, park and dissolve. Separate durable membership from the one-active-Air runtime pointer. Freeze hashed one-time invite entropy, expiry, rate and redaction; role and confirmation authority; simultaneous join and capacity conflicts; Air and membership statuses; lazy parked behavior and deferred GC; creator or role dissolve authority; join or leave during track, overlay and prepare; current and saved Air read models; default invite, overlay, queue and replace policies and policy-change effect on accepted work. Map approach, accept, decline and apart aliases and define additive cutover and rollback authority.

## Acceptance Criteria
One reviewed contract fixes endpoint and callback fields, statuses, authorization, audit, one-active conflict, capacity, invite security, saved versus active state, join catch-up, leave or dissolve audio behavior, policy defaults, aliases and rollback cutover. A joining primary must confirm, parked rooms do not auto-delete or run audio, accepted target snapshots do not change retroactively, and target or inbox details remain owned by their story.
