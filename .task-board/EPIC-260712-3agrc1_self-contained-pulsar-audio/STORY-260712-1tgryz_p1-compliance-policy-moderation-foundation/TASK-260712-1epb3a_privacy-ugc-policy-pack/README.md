# Author the reviewed RU and EN privacy and UGC policy pack

## Description
Produce legally reviewable source documents that accurately describe the implemented accountless private-audio product and its moderation controls.

## Scope
From the dated Store matrix and approved legal inputs, author equivalent RU and EN privacy policy, Terms of Service, Content Guidelines and concise upload-rights notice. Cover explicit microphone capture, local self-test, accountless actor and recovery credentials, uploaded audio and metadata, optional Telegram and Spotify integrations, processing, security, target-limited sharing, retention, deletion, backups, reports and operator evidence access, contact and user controls. State honestly that Phase 1 is not E2EE and is not a public commercial-music rebroadcast service. Version the documents, capture legal review status and map each paragraph to product behavior.

## Acceptance Criteria
The policy pack satisfies the current official privacy and UGC matrix, contains no placeholders or unsupported deletion, recovery, encryption, moderation or availability promise, and has semantically equivalent RU and EN text. The file-upload rights reminder and report or removal process are clear. A reviewer can trace every factual statement to a shipped control, approved operational input or explicit limitation.
