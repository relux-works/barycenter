# Approve Phase 2 codec candidate, legal and supply-chain gates

## Description
Obtain qualified AAC/LGPL counsel disposition and an implementation-independent review of the exact Phase 2 codec replacement candidate. Proposed engineering default: bundled FFmpeg 8.1.2 minimal shared, package-local and dark-only, with no runtime download or production activation until every hard gate passes.

## Scope
Ivan Oparin selects qualified counsel and a technically qualified reviewer who implemented none of the codec/package work. Counsel decides AAC product/hosted-conversion and LGPL distribution obligations for exact markets. The reviewer inspects the exact replacement commit, binaries, runtime SBOM, source/build receipts, licenses, signatures, notarization, vulnerability closure, hostile-input containment and raw no-averaging hard-gate matrix; then records accept or reject. Reversible candidate-neutral engineering may continue; production playback and Phase 2 promotion remain withheld.

## Acceptance Criteria
The task records counsel identity/date/disposition, reviewer identity and independence, exact reviewed commit and binary hashes, selected Windows-plus-macOS combination, closure and re-review of P2-CODEC-SUPPLY-001 through 006, complete SBOM/source/build/signing/notarization evidence, and an explicit Ivan Oparin proceed or hold decision. No unsigned, downloaded, license-ambiguous or vulnerability-unresolved decoder is activated.
