# Persist and enforce versioned Air policies

## Description
Make invite, overlay, queue and replace authorization explicit, auditable and subordinate to local recipient controls.

## Scope
Persist frozen defaults and versioned mutations for who may invite Barycenters, send overlay, enqueue tracks and replace main program. Enforce role and Air policy at lifecycle and common transmission entry points before target acceptance, including legacy aliases and Telegram. Audit actor, old and new values and accepted time. Policy changes affect future requests and contract-defined pending work only, never broaden an immutable accepted target snapshot. Local DND, block, local volume ceiling and actor disable always win.

## Acceptance Criteria
Unauthorized commands fail with stable privacy-safe reasons at every transport; allowed actions survive restart. Pairwise migrated Airs receive documented defaults. Concurrent policy changes serialize, audit is complete, accepted target ACL never expands retroactively, and no alias, app, bot or automation path bypasses local controls.
