# Prove Air lifecycle, migration, isolation and capacity

## Description
Close the Air story with adversarial concurrency, production-shaped rollback, runtime and initial load evidence while naming stream and inbox dependencies honestly.

## Scope
Test create, saved versus active switch, hashed invite replay and concurrent consume, joining-primary confirmation, role and policy races, limits, three-Barycenter peer union, no duplicate or transitive delivery, offline operation, join during track or overlay, leave or dissolve during prepare and playback, lazy parking, restart and alias behavior. Fault-inject backfill and authority cutover, run previous-binary rollback, and script eight Barycenters or twenty Pulsars with resource and duplicate-command metrics. Use Windows and macOS UI or contract fixtures and secure Telegram callbacks. Defer full track catch-up and target ACL proof to their integration gates.

## Acceptance Criteria
Automated evidence proves Air state and one-active invariants, exact B2-B4 lifecycle behavior available within this story, no duplicate or transitive runtime, no invite or role bypass, lazy park and clean leave, additive migration and non-dual rollback. The initial 8 or 20 load either passes named limits or records a concrete blocker; streamed-track catch-up and target-security dependencies are explicit rather than falsely marked passed.
