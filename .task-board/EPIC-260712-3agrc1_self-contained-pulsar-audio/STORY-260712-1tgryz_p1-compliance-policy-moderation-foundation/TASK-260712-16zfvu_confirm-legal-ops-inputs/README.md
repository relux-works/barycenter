# Confirm legal, support and submission inputs

## Description
Resolve company and operational facts that cannot be safely invented in code or policy copy.

## Scope
Obtain and record approved Relux Works legal identity and jurisdiction, data-controller and privacy contact, support and moderation mailbox ownership, public policy and support URLs, hosting owner, target markets and age positioning, governing-law or dispute-language decision, operator roster and response/escalation owner, Partner Center roles, and who authorizes the final external submission. Record whether legal counsel will review RU and EN policy text and which facts must remain configurable rather than checked into source.

## Acceptance Criteria
Every public policy, in-product link, moderation runbook and Partner Center field has a real approved value and accountable owner. No placeholder email, address, jurisdiction, SLA, legal promise or submission authority reaches production. Missing decisions stay an explicit blocker and are presented to the user in one concise checklist.
