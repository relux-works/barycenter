# Independently review privacy moderation and Store claims

## Description
Audit the final user-visible privacy and compliance behavior separately from implementation and cryptographic code review.

## Scope
Review self-contained onboarding, microphone indicators, protected versus plaintext labels, silent-downgrade prevention, metadata disclosure, irrecoverable key-loss wording, Telegram boundaries, report evidence consent, operator access and TTL, delete or revoke limits, UGC report and block, privacy policy, IARC, localized Store copy, screenshots and certification notes. Reproduce actor and target isolation and evidence-access cases on the root-reviewed build. No placeholder legal, support or Partner Center authority may be invented.

## Acceptance Criteria
Claims exactly match proven flags and capability matrix, report evidence and moderation access are lawful and auditable, no unsupported E2EE or erase claim remains and all critical or high privacy or Store findings are closed. Missing legal inputs or submission authority remain explicit blockers.
