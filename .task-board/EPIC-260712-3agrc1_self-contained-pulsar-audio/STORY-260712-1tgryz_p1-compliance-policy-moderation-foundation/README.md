# P1 Policy and moderation foundation

## Description
Create the legal-policy publication moderation service and operator baseline that Telegram and platform compliance surfaces consume.

## Scope
Verify current Store policy, collect real legal and support inputs, write and publish privacy, terms and UGC guidance, implement actor-scoped report, delete and disable control plane and document the moderation mailbox and runbook. This story owns foundations only; platform and Telegram UIs, final screenshots, independent reviews and Partner Center submission remain in STORY-260712-1i0doc.

## Acceptance Criteria
Real policy pages and support contacts are published, the canonical moderation backend is authorization-tested and auditable, and an operator can execute report, delete and disable without invented values. Downstream clients can integrate without a dependency cycle; no Store submission or A1-A8 claim is made here.
