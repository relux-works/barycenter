# Publish stable policy and support pages

## Description
Make the approved privacy, terms, content-guideline and support material publicly reachable without an account and consistently linked from every product surface.

## Scope
Publish versioned RU and EN privacy policy, Terms, Content Guidelines, upload-rights summary and support or moderation contact at approved HTTPS URLs under the Pulsar or Relux domain. Include effective and updated dates, language switch, stable anchors and a non-authenticated availability check. Wire links from Windows, macOS, Telegram help, website and Store source metadata, and document cache or rollback behavior so a release never points to missing or draft text.

## Acceptance Criteria
Every packaged and Store link opens the approved locale or a clear fallback over HTTPS without login. Pages match the reviewed source hashes and current product behavior, expose working privacy and support contacts, and remain available during the certification run. Automated link checks and a release checklist prevent dead, draft or mismatched policy URLs.
