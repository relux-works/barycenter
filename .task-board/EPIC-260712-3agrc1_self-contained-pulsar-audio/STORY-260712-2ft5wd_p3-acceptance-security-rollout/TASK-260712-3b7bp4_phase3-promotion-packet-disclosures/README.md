# Assemble the Phase 3 promotion or hold packet

## Description
Assemble but do not self-authorize the complete release packet from frozen raw evidence and reviews.

## Scope
Update acceptance and runbook docs, privacy and moderation deltas, Store and IARC copy, certification notes, capability matrix, metadata and recovery limits, exact feature flags and cohorts, rollback commands, external and independent reviewer reports, beta log, incidents and C1-C7 artifact index. Provide independent promote or hold recommendations for live_ptt, e2ee_media, soundboard_cues and automation. Use real legal support and Partner Center values only.

## Acceptance Criteria
A reviewer can trace every claim and gate to raw artifacts and reviewed build hashes without chat archaeology. Partial promotion is explicit and safe, missing authority remains a blocker and no feature, E2EE, deletion or recovery claim exceeds evidence. The packet awaits the separate root final audit.
